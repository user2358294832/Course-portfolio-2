## 📜 Senaryo (Scenario)

Sahne, bir mentor ve öğrencisi arasındaki "geçmişin onarılamazlığı" üzerine kısa ama vurucu bir diyaloga odaklanmaktadır:

> **The Mentor:** "Time doesn't heal, Julian. It only hides the truth."
> 
> **Julian:** *(Whispering)* "I thought I could fix it. I thought I could bring back what was lost."
> 
> **The Mentor:** "Some things are broken by design. Leave it, and walk away."
